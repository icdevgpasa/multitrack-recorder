NagrajTo is a multitrack studio in your pocket. Record guitar, then bass, vocals and harmonies while listening to everything you've already laid down. Latency compensation and per-headphone calibration keep every take in time.

RECORD
• Overdub new tracks while hearing the previous ones
• Metronome with count-in and adjustable tempo
• Live headphone monitoring with reverb
• Mic input gain and one-tap track normalize
• Latency calibration for the speaker and each pair of headphones

MIX AND EDIT
• Volume, pan, solo and mute per track
• Trim, fade in/out and nudge a take in time
• Non-destructive: you can always restore the original take

EXPORT
• The whole song as M4A or 24-bit WAV
• Every track separately (WAV) for your DAW
• Share via AirDrop, Files and messaging apps

LOOKS
• Live waveforms, a spectrum visualizer and a background that pulses with your music

PRIVACY
Your recordings stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Tip: record with headphones so the mic doesn't pick up the other tracks.
